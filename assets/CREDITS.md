# assets/

These two files are **not** my work and are **not** covered by the repository's MIT license.

| File | Author | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `MENU1920.BMP` | Ferdinand Zeppelin | 1920×1080 main-menu background, 8-bit BMP |
| `1920X1080.WAD` | Ferdinand Zeppelin | interface panels (top bar, side panel) laid out for 1920×1080 |

They have circulated freely in the Commandos modding community since 2013 and were
distributed, among other places, in Kruulos's `Commandos_BEL_BCD_Ultimate_Fixes.rar`
linked from the Steam guide
[1920 x 1080p / High Resolution Guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=395264353),
and referenced by the
[commandosmod widescreen tutorial](https://sites.google.com/site/commandosmod/tutorials/bel_widescreen).

They are included here only so that the scripts work without sending anyone chasing
links that have been dead for years. They are derived from artwork belonging to
Pyro Studios / Eidos. If you are a rights holder and would prefer they were not
hosted here, open an issue and they will be removed — the scripts can be pointed at
a local copy instead.

The map-border technique the scripts implement (`POLY "WIDESCREENFIX"` with tiles at
brightness −20) is also Ferdinand Zeppelin's, from `ResolutionFix_VOLfix.zip`. No file
from that archive is redistributed here; the scripts generate the block themselves from
each map's own dimensions.
